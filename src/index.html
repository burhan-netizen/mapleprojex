The Maple Way

Not just getting it done. Getting it right.

Clean lines, solid finishes, a well-kept site and straight talk the whole way through. That's the standard on every job, big or small.

Clean lines

Measure twice, cut once. Square, level and plumb, because you'll see it every day.

Solid finishes

Good timber, proper fixings and joins that stay tight. Built to last, not to pass a quick look.

A kept site

Floors and benches protected, mess cleared as we go. It's your home while we work in it.

Straight talk

You'll know what's happening, what's next and when. No chasing, no surprises at the end.

FAQ

Good questions. Straight answers.

What areas do you cover?
We work across Sydney's Eastern Suburbs. If you're close by but not sure you're in range, call Greg on 0404 348 901 and ask.
What kind of jobs do you take on?
Custom carpentry (framing, stairs, trims and finishing), timber decks and outdoor spaces, kitchens and joinery, bathroom and interior carpentry, custom timber gates and screens, and project management for bigger jobs.
Can you manage the whole job, not just the carpentry?
Yes. Project management is part of what we do: organising the job, coordinating what needs to happen and keeping it moving, so you're not left chasing people.
